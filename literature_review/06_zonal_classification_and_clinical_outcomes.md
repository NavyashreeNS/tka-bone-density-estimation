### 1.1 The Knee Society Roentgenographic Evaluation System (Ewald 1989)
Ewald formalized 7 zones underneath the tibial component on anteroposterior (AP) and lateral radiographs:
- **AP View**:
  - Zone 1: Medial plateau under tray.
  - Zone 2: Lateral plateau under tray.
  - Zone 3: Medial keel/stem contact.
  - Zone 4: Lateral keel/stem contact.
  - Zone 5: Distal stem tip.
  - Zone 6: Inferior cortical margin.
  - Zone 7: Peripheral cortex.
- *Limitation*: 2D radiographs conflate radiolucent lines ($> 1\text{ mm}$ indicates impending loosening) with projection angle misalignment.

### 1.2 Volumetric 3D Adaptation for Quantitative CT
In the proposed pipeline, Ewald zones are extruded and geometrically bounded in 3D:
- **Zone 1 (Medial Sub-Plate)**: Bounded by the medial half of the tibial tray, extending $5\text{ mm}$ distally.
- **Zone 2 (Lateral Sub-Plate)**: Bounded by the lateral half of the tibial tray, extending $5\text{ mm}$ distally.
- **Zone 3 (Anterior Cortex)**: Anterior quadrant cortex and sub-cortical cancellous bone.
- **Zone 4 (Posterior Cortex)**: Posterior quadrant cortex and sub-cortical cancellous bone.
- **Zone 5 (Peri-Keel Cancellous)**: Cancellous bone immediately adjacent to central stem/keel fins.
- **Zone 6 (Distal Stem Apex)**: $10\text{ mm}$ sphere centered on the distal stem tip.
- **Zone 7 (Metaphyseal/Diaphyseal Baseline)**: Deep cortical reference cylinder $> 15\text{ mm}$ below stem apex.

## 2. Clinical Outcomes & Failure Correlation
- **Radiostereometric Analysis (RSA)**: Migration of $> 0.2\text{ mm}$ within the first post-operative year indicates high risk of late aseptic loosening (Ryd et al., 1995; Pijls et al., 2012). Bone density loss in Zones 1 and 2 directly correlates with continuous mechanical migration.
- **Osteolysis vs. Stress Shielding Differentiation**:
  - *Stress Shielding*: Diffuse, symmetric density loss occurring within the first 6–12 months, followed by stabilization.
  - *Progressive Osteolysis*: Focal, non-linear bone loss with sharp density gradients ($\nabla \rho_v > 250\text{ mg/cm}^3/\text{mm}$), expanding continuously beyond 2 years due to polyethylene wear debris.
