# Stage 6 Milestone: 3D Zonal Density Mapping & Radial Isochrones

## Objective
Spatially aggregate quantitative bone density ($v\text{BMD}$) into standardized anatomical volumes (7-Zone Proximal Tibial System) and geometric radial distance shells ($0\text{--}5\text{ mm}$), enabling direct comparison across patients and implant designs.

## Zonal System (Volumetric Adaptation of Ewald/Kozinn)
- **Zone 1**: Medial sub-plate trabecular block ($0\text{--}5\text{ mm}$ beneath medial tray)
- **Zone 2**: Lateral sub-plate trabecular block ($0\text{--}5\text{ mm}$ beneath lateral tray)
- **Zone 3**: Anterior cortical margin
- **Zone 4**: Posterior cortical margin
- **Zone 5**: Peri-keel central cancellous bed
- **Zone 6**: Distal stem tip apex zone
- **Zone 7**: Deep diaphyseal baseline reference

## Distance-Isochrone Shells
- **Shell A**: $0.0\text{--}0.5\text{ mm}$ (Immediate interface fixation / biological ingrowth)
- **Shell B**: $0.5\text{--}1.0\text{ mm}$
- **Shell C**: $1.0\text{--}2.0\text{ mm}$
- **Shell D**: $2.0\text{--}3.0\text{ mm}$
- **Shell E**: $3.0\text{--}5.0\text{ mm}$ (Surrounding host bone)
